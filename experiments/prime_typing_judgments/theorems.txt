Native implementation handoff
-----------------------------

The remaining native contract must connect one resolved definition identity
and revision to ordinary execution, conversion, dependency queries and proof
reuse. StructuralTypingReplayDependencies and StructuralConversionCodeDependencies
already prove exact verdict reuse under their stated agreements. This tranche
does not infer that agreement from a seal, revision counter or successful
demonstration. The two preserved same-source/conflicting-conversion fixtures
are open findings until this connection is implemented and qualified.
