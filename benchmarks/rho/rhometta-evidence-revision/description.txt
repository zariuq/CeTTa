rhometta-evidence-revision -- a truth value that is actually computed
====================================================================

THE IDEA
  Two agents each watch some ravens and form an opinion about "ravens are black" (written (Inheritance raven black))
  -- a strength (fraction seen black) and a confidence (how much evidence backs
  it). They happen to share two observations. If you merge their opinions
  naively, that shared evidence gets counted twice and the confidence comes out
  too high. Overlap-corrected revision discounts the shared observations and
  reports the honest confidence. This is the one rho demo where a truth value
  belongs: it is COUNTED FROM OBSERVATIONS, not hand-set (a typing or a proof, by
  contrast, is simply true or not).

WHAT YOU SEE
  Same strength either way (0.75 = 3 of 4 seen black). The honest difference is
  the confidence:
      naive (double-counts the shared evidence):  0.889
      overlap-corrected (the honest value):       0.857

  Two shapes of the same computation:
    Variant B -- both agents run in parallel; the revision happens after the run.
    Variant A -- the revision happens AT the final rendezvous (communication is
                 computation): the first agent's result is carried into the
                 payload that revises it against the second.

  Each agent's opinion is computed at COMM time by a deferred MeTTa payload
  (count observations -> build the stv), not read off a table.

RUN
  ./cetta --quiet --profile extended --lang he benchmarks/rho/rhometta-evidence-revision/example.metta
