rhometta ECAN attention spreading

Round-based ECAN-style importance (STI) spreading on a small Hebbian graph,
following the diffusion dynamics of Goertzel et al.'s Economic Attention
Networks (literature/Goertzel_Ben/Hyperon_Drafts/ECAN_final.pdf): per cycle
the STI vector is multiplied by a LEFT-STOCHASTIC diffusion matrix built from
the Hebbian connection weights with a MaxSpread constraint -- each atom keeps
(1 - MaxSpread) of its STI and spreads at most MaxSpread, distributed
proportionally to its outgoing Hebbian weights (atoms with no outgoing links
keep everything).  Left-stochasticity gives the ECAN economy invariant:
TOTAL STI IS CONSERVED ACROSS ROUNDS, which the demo checks each round.

rho orchestrates the rounds: one spreading round = a confluent rhometta farm
in which each atom's new STI is computed by a deferred MeTTa payload at its
own COMM rendezvous (frozen shared Hebbian KB + the current STI vector carried
as data in the payload).  MeTTa drives the iteration, re-embedding the
extracted vector into the next round's payloads.  Under num-threads N the
per-atom updates of a round genuinely run in parallel.

Also shown: the attentional-focus readout (atoms with STI above a threshold)
shifting across rounds as importance flows from the stimulated atom along
the Hebbian structure.

Scope note: this is the spreading/diffusion kernel of ECAN with its
conservation economy.  The rent-and-wage step (s_i - rent + wages, charged
to the attentional focus) and Hebbian weight learning are deliberately
omitted here; full ECAN with mutable per-atom state belongs to the later
actor-owned-space slice of rhometta.

Run:
  ./cetta --profile extended --lang he \
      benchmarks/rho/rhometta-ecan-spreading/example.metta
