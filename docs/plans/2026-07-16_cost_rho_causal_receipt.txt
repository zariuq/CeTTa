Canonical object
================

The primary account of one pure cost-rho run is a finite, located, measured
pomset of firing occurrences.  It is not a list modulo permutations and it is
not a set of distinct costs.

Each event has:

* an occurrence identity;
* one consumption arc for every consumed resource occurrence that was produced
  by an earlier firing;
* a nonempty finite collection of funding contributions;
* a raw spend equal to the multiset sum of those contributions.

A funding contribution is a pair of an opaque funding-surface identity and a
raw signature multiset.  Funding surfaces and signatures are different types.
The runtime emits one contribution for every selected located purse head, so a
split cover retains several contributions without aggregating occurrences.

The causal order is the reflexive-transitive closure of the support of the
consumption multigraph.  Arc multiplicity is retained separately.  Independence
is causal incomparability and is never supplied as an extra relation.

For a finite event region U, the operational measure is

  mu(U) = sum(event in U, rawSpend(event))

in the free commutative monoid of cost signatures.  Effort-object balances,
quantale totals, and future numerical readings are folds of this measure.


CeTTa surface and encoding
==========================

`lib/lts/rho/cost.metta` exposes:

  (lts:rho:cost:causal-trace STATE)
  (lts:rho:cost:causal-prefix REDUCTION-FUEL SEARCH-FUEL STATE)

It selects one canonical pure cost-rho run and returns:

  (lts:rho:cost:receipt
    (EVENT ...)
    FINAL-STATE)

Each event has the form:

  (lts:rho:cost:event
    EVENT-ID
    (CAUSE-ID ...)
    ((lts:rho:cost:funding FUNDING-SURFACE RAW-CONTRIBUTION) ...)
    RAW-SPEND)

The ordered event sequence is a presentation of the pomset, not the primary
semantic object.  Repeated cause IDs denote different consumed occurrences
from the same producer and are not collapsed.

The bounded form reports `quiescent`, `fuel-exhausted`, or `search-exhausted`.
Quiescence is emitted only after exhaustive candidate and cover search proves
there is no funded successor.  Reduction fuel is checked first, so zero firing
allowance performs no cover search and reports fuel exhaustion.  Causal records
are output-only, so there is no causal-record parser boundary.  Malformed input
is checked at the existing cost-term boundary.


Operational provenance
======================

The traced machine maintains an internal sidecar for each normalized top-level
cost component:

  (term, optional producing EventId)

Initial components have no producer.  Candidate COMM firings are obtained from
the existing cost reducer, augmented internally with the exact participant
component indices, selected located-purse indices, and contractum.  The trace
selects a candidate before the existing `(residual, spent)` frontier
deduplication.

For each selected firing:

1. append the producer of every consumed top-level participant, when present;
2. append the producer of every selected purse occurrence, when present;
3. retain repeated producers in the cause list;
4. assign the fresh event ID to every top-level component of the contractum;
5. assign the fresh event ID to every exposed selected purse tail;
6. retain the producer of every untouched component;
7. normalize and sort terms together with their sidecars;
8. check that erasing the sidecar gives exactly the existing reducer result.

The local EventId backend is a monotone per-trace counter.  It makes every
cause smaller than its effect and therefore makes emission order a linear
extension by construction.  Identity is never inferred from term equality,
signature equality, normalized residual state, or funding location.


Purse serialization
===================

A selected stack tail is an output of the consuming event.  Consequently, a
later firing funded by that tail has the previous spend as a cause.  Spending
from one stack is therefore serialized even when the funded processes are
otherwise unrelated.

Withdrawals from one purse lineage are ordered, while purses at independent
surfaces do not acquire a false causal edge from emission order.  A future
change from stacks to token multisets would be a separate semantic decision and
would require token-occurrence provenance.


Event identity upgrade seam
===========================

The Lean emission type is polymorphic in its EventId, and the semantic receipt
is polymorphic in its event carrier.  Receipt labels, arc multiplicities,
ranks, raw measures, and downstream valuations are invariant under injective
identity relabeling.

A content digest alone is not a valid EventId: equal-content firings are
distinct occurrences and must not collapse.  If receipts cross a trust
boundary, the designated secure backend is a Merkle-DAG identity committing to
an occurrence discriminator, the canonically serialized event label, and its
parent cause identities.  Canonical serialization and collision resistance are
external protocol obligations and are deliberately not assumed by the pure
receipt theory.


Lean semantics and valuations
==============================

The owned modules are:

* `Mettapedia/Languages/ProcessCalculi/RhoCalculus/Cost/Receipt.lean`;
* `Mettapedia/Languages/ProcessCalculi/RhoCalculus/Cost/Valuation.lean`;
* `Mettapedia/Languages/ProcessCalculi/RhoCalculus/Cost/Examples.lean`.

`CausalReceipt` requires a finite event carrier.  Its direct arcs carry natural
multiplicity and a rank certificate; the generated reachability relation is
proved to be a partial order.

`ReceiptEmission.Valid` requires unique IDs and requires every cause ID to name
an earlier record.  Every valid emission constructs a receipt on its occurrence
positions.  Construction preserves event count, labels, arc multiplicity, and
the exact fold of emitted raw spends.  Emission order is proved to linearize
causal order.

Additive and multiplicative valuations are constructed from the universal fold
of the free signature multiset.  Their sequential, parallel, relabeling, and
per-location restriction laws are theorems derived from the folds.  No
valuation-law record is assumed.  Effort-object remaining budgets and quantale
totals are downstream definitions over those constructed valuations.


Executable boundary
===================

The CeTTa fixtures pin:

* a two-COMM causal chain, including two retained arcs from one producer;
* a produced split-endpoint COMM, including both endpoint arcs and its purse-tail arc;
* two independent firings with no false edge from emission order;
* two equal-spend firings preserved as distinct events;
* actual opaque funding surfaces, including split covers;
* quiescent and malformed cost-term inputs;
* zero-fuel and search-exhaustion separation;
* a 20-purse, 10-head combinatorial cover without eager materialization.

These fixtures check the concrete encoding and reducer behavior.  They are not
a universal theorem relating compiled C to Lean.  Such a theorem still requires
checked extraction or a systematic cross-language semantics bridge.


Deferred join
=============

Rhometa payload evaluation remains outside the cost grammar.  Pricing it is
gated on spaces-as-channels: ownership, mobility, isolation, and true parallel
space semantics must exist before their events can be priced.  The future join
adds payload evaluation as a priced ownership/computation event without changing
the causal-receipt carrier.
